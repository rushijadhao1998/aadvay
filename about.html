About Us

The work of 'Aadvay Multipurpose Foundation' is not just limited to social welfare, but also has a special focus on educational development. By starting guidance centers, the organization is striving to make unemployed youth, both male and female, and women employable. The organization provides training in various arts and skills. Through vocational training in fields like tailoring, computers, beauty culture, bakery, and handicrafts, the focus is on making women and youth self-reliant. The organization's policy is to promote the all-round development of the youth by starting 'youth leadership development camps' and 'gymnasiums'. Other important initiatives of the organization include organizing 'health check-up camps' to provide free medicines, offering health facilities to the disabled and physically/mentally challenged individuals, and disseminating agricultural knowledge and information to farmers. The organization is also making special efforts for the development of athletes.

Aadvay Multipurpose Foundation

Vision

To create a healthy and self-reliant society by achieving the holistic development of every section of the community.

Mission

To empower the underprivileged and marginalized sections of society by implementing various schemes and initiatives to bring about positive change in the fields of education, health, vocational training, youth welfare, and women and child development.

Objectives

• To start and run educational institutions (from nursery to senior college).

• To establish medical, engineering, dental, and Ayurvedic colleges.

• To start training centers for competitive exams and pre-police recruitment.

• To implement schemes for social welfare, youth welfare, and women and child development.

• To provide various vocational trainings (tailoring, computer, beauty culture, bakery, etc.).

• To organize youth leadership development camps and start gymnasiums.

• To provide health facilities for needy individuals and organize health check-up camps.

• To provide agricultural knowledge and information to farmers.

• To encourage athletes and help them reach national and international levels.
